 🟢 Exit Protocol — Clinical Sign-Off & Safe Exit
 Doctor Nurse Health Coordinator 
 Applies to ALL exit requests at any stage. No patient is ever cancelled without this process completed.
 Step 1 — Pause and acknowledge: Nurse contacts patient within 5 days of any exit intent. Listen first — no pressure, no sales, no judgement.
 Step 2 — Understand the reason: Nurse asks open questions to identify root cause — clinical concern, cost, personal circumstances, dissatisfaction, or achieved goal.
 Step 3 — Clinical safety check: Nurse confirms patient is medically safe to pause or stop medication. If on a higher dose — tapering plan discussed. Doctor review required before any abrupt stop.
 Step 4 — Doctor sign-off: Any patient stopping GLP-1 medication must have formal Doctor review. Tapering or cessation plan documented in Semble EHR. Doctor co-signs the exit.
 Step 5 — Alternative solution offered: Health Coordinator presents the most appropriate alternative — pause, downgrade, standalone prescription, return at any time.
 Step 6 — Decision recorded: Full outcome logged in HubSpot (reason + outcome: Retained / Paused / Downgraded / Exited) and Semble clinical notes updated same day.
 Step 7 — Re-engagement pathway: HubSpot sequence activated for all non-retained outcomes. Patient never deleted from CRM. Open door communicated clearly.

 🟢 Exit Protocol — Clinical Sign-Off & Safe Exit
 Doctor Nurse Health Coordinator 
 Applies to ALL exit requests at any stage. No patient is ever cancelled without this process completed.
 Step 1 — Pause and acknowledge: Nurse contacts patient within 5 days of any exit intent. Listen first — no pressure, no sales, no judgement.
 Step 2 — Understand the reason: Nurse asks open questions to identify root cause — clinical concern, cost, personal circumstances, dissatisfaction, or achieved goal.
 Step 3 — Clinical safety check: Nurse confirms patient is medically safe to pause or stop medication. If on a higher dose — tapering plan discussed. Doctor review required before any abrupt stop.
 Step 4 — Doctor sign-off: Any patient stopping GLP-1 medication must have formal Doctor review. Tapering or cessation plan documented in Semble EHR. Doctor co-signs the exit.
 Step 5 — Alternative solution offered: Health Coordinator presents the most appropriate alternative — pause, downgrade, standalone prescription, return at any time. No hard sell — patient led.
 Step 6 — Decision recorded: Full outcome logged in HubSpot (reason category + outcome: Retained / Paused / Downgraded / Exited) and Semble clinical notes updated same day.
 Step 7 — Re-engagement pathway: HubSpot sequence activated for all non-retained outcomes. Patient never deleted from CRM. Open door communicated clearly.

 💬 Feedback Touchpoints — this stage
 Monthly Nurse check-in — close of every call — Nurse asks: How are you feeling about the programme this month? Are you getting what you need? Nurse logs sentiment in Semble + HubSpot: Positive / Neutral / At-Risk.

 Monthly automated pulse check (HubSpot) — 2-question email sent on the 15th of each month: (1) How supported do you feel right now? 1–5. (2) Is there anything you need from us this month? Responses routed to Nurse task if score ≤2.

 🟢 Exit Protocol — Clinical Sign-Off & Safe Exit
 Doctor Nurse Health Coordinator 
 Applies to ALL exit requests at any stage. No patient is ever cancelled without this process completed.
 Step 1 — Pause and acknowledge: Nurse contacts patient within 5 days of any exit intent. Listen first — no pressure, no sales, no judgement.
 Step 2 — Understand the reason: Nurse asks open questions to identify root cause — clinical concern, cost, personal circumstances, dissatisfaction, or achieved goal.
 Step 3 — Clinical safety check: Nurse confirms patient is medically safe to pause or stop medication. If on a higher dose — tapering plan discussed. Doctor review required before any abrupt stop.
 Step 4 — Doctor sign-off: Any patient stopping GLP-1 medication must have formal Doctor review. Tapering or cessation plan documented in Semble EHR. Doctor co-signs the exit.
 Step 5 — Alternative solution offered: Health Coordinator presents the most appropriate alternative — pause, downgrade, standalone prescription, return at any time. No hard sell — patient led.
 Step 6 — Decision recorded: Full outcome logged in HubSpot (reason category + outcome: Retained / Paused / Downgraded / Exited) and Semble clinical notes updated same day.
 Step 7 — Re-engagement pathway: HubSpot sequence activated for all non-retained outcomes. Patient never deleted from CRM. Open door communicated clearly.
